## How it started

The idea took shape right when AI was becoming everywhere. Feissal was running marketing and sales for an agency, Marouan was leading IT. Two different jobs, two different temperaments, and an obvious complementarity very early on. Two friends, almost brothers, with good days, rough days, and endless cups of coffee.

The question came up one evening: what if we built something of our own? Something that truly reflects who we are.

The project matured slowly. Adam joined the adventure. Today the technical team has three people, surrounded by salespeople, and what was once just an idea has become real.

## How we work

Three words capture our approach: Build. Grow. Automate. We build, we grow, we automate.

That motto comes from years in the field and a simple conviction: know what to do, where to do it, and when. No website just to have a website. No system just to have a system. We design tools that give you back time and money, and take repetitive tasks off your plate.

Our method, in four steps:

1. Clarify the problem and the expected outcome.
2. Build the simplest version that proves the value.
3. Measure, correct, then industrialise.
4. Document what worked so you don't start from scratch.

## Who we serve

Moroccan small and medium businesses first, with strong roots in Essaouira and Marrakech. That's our ground, we know its realities and its ecosystem. We also work with French-speaking clients in France, Belgium and beyond.
